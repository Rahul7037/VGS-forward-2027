FOUR PATHS TO BETTER THINKING

Choose your challenge.

Each competition develops a different capability. The progression is intentional—from checking AI claims to building and defending more demanding systems.

THE VERIFIER01
CLASSES VI–VII · 2 students

AI Detective

A convincing AI answer is not necessarily a correct one. Investigate claims, inspect evidence, explain uncertainty and change your conclusion when the facts change.

RESPONSIBLE AI IN ACTION

AI is a collaborator.
Not the judge.

For the making competitions, a supervised AI Assistance Desk helps students ask their own questions, assess suggestions and independently check the results.

Important distinction: AI Detective generally investigates prepared AI outputs without an open assistance desk. Senior Tech can optionally adopt a standardised AI integration mode if the technical environment is ready.

HOW THE DESK OPERATES
Students write their own prompts
An authorised adult enters the prompt unchanged
Responses are displayed and logged
Students decide what to accept or reject
Final work must survive independent testing

Prompt allowances and timing differ by competition. The approved SOP governs operations.

BEFORE THE EVENT
