AI Detective.Question the answer.

A convincing AI answer is not necessarily a correct one. Investigate claims, inspect evidence, explain uncertainty and change your conclusion when the facts change.

MISSION

A digital mystery of claims, evidence and responsible judgement.

Students receive a case containing AI-generated statements and a collection of evidence. Some claims may be correct, some misleading, and others impossible to establish. A timed evidence update requires students to reconsider their findings.

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN

A capability, not just a competition.

Each phase requires students to explain their own decisions. A plausible output without validation does not count as a proven result.

Investigate

Separate an AI claim from its supporting evidence.

Verify

Find contradictory facts, missing context and misleading comparisons.

Defend

Explain a conclusion and its degree of certainty.

Revise

Update the conclusion when new evidence appears.

THE STUDENT JOURNEY

From brief to evidence.

01

Inspect the case

Read the AI output and identify specific claims to test.

02

Check the evidence

Compare each claim with the common evidence provided.

03

Record your verdict

Mark supported, contradicted or not yet proven, with reasons.

04

Respond to new facts

Review the surprise evidence and defend any revision.

AI ACCESS

Who can ask AI—and when?

No routine open AI Assistance Desk. A controlled AI Test Bench may be used only where the selected mystery explicitly requires prompt testing.

PILOT CHALLENGE

What will VGS test first?

Students receive a case containing AI-generated statements and a collection of evidence. Some claims may be correct, some misleading, and others impossible to establish. A timed evidence update requires students to reconsider their findings.

HOW SUCCESS IS EVALUATED

Show the evidence.

The published rules will include criterion weights, standard test methods and judging arrangements. These are the present assessment priorities, not a final inter-school score sheet.

  • Evidence-based conclusions
  • Source evaluation and uncertainty
  • Logical and numerical reasoning
  • Ability to revise with new facts
  • Independent explanation

Before final approval: The final case and answer key must be independently set and sealed. The three candidate mysteries in the pilot pack are not confirmed final questions.
