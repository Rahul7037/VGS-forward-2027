MISSION

Make a reliable technology system, not just a convincing demo.

The first pilot candidate is Mission Control, a scheduling validator for a fictional festival. Teams build rules, identify conflicts, repair assignments and revalidate after a shared disruption.

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN

A capability, not just a competition.

Each phase requires students to explain their own decisions. A plausible output without validation does not count as a proven result.

Model

Represent data, rules and trade-offs clearly.

Implement

Build reliable functionality with an appropriate tool.

Validate

Test edge cases and reject invalid outcomes.

Defend

Respond to a new requirement and explain design decisions.

THE STUDENT JOURNEY

From brief to evidence.

01

Understand the data

Inspect activities, rooms, time slots and constraints.

02

Build the validator

Create a usable way to check and revise assignments.

03

Test and explain

Use common acceptance tests; record where AI advice helped.

04

Recover from disruption

Add the changed constraints, repair the schedule and prove validity.

AI ACCESS

Who can ask AI—and when?

Mode A: two assisted-engineering consultations, up to two prompts each. Mode B: optional standardised AI integration service, only after technical and fairness testing. The modes must not share a ranking.

PILOT CHALLENGE

What will VGS test first?

The first pilot candidate is Mission Control, a scheduling validator for a fictional festival. Teams build rules, identify conflicts, repair assignments and revalidate after a shared disruption.

HOW SUCCESS IS EVALUATED

Show the evidence.

The published rules will include criterion weights, standard test methods and judging arrangements. These are the present assessment priorities, not a final inter-school score sheet.

  • Sound engineering and architecture
  • Correct validation and edge cases
  • Testing evidence
  • Recovery after changed requirements
  • Responsible AI use and technical defence

Before final approval: Automatic scheduling, RAG and agents are not compulsory. A production-grade shared AI gateway would need separate development and approval.
