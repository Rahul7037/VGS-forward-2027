MISSION

Build, measure, improve — let evidence decide.

The first pilot candidate is Rainwater Rescue: build a gravity-fed collection device using limited materials and evaluate real water transfer. Teams compare repeated measurements and adapt to a changed condition.

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN

A capability, not just a competition.

Each phase requires students to explain their own decisions. A plausible output without validation does not count as a proven result.

Hypothesise

Compare possible approaches and predict outcomes.

Make

Construct with a controlled material kit.

Measure

Record the same metric across fair tests.

Improve

Explain what changed and whether the evidence supports it.

THE STUDENT JOURNEY

From brief to evidence.

01

Predict and sketch

Consider physical principles and choose a testable design.

02

Construct and measure

Build a device and record initial performance.

03

Ask AI, then verify

Use the measurements to seek improvement ideas; run a comparable retest.

04

Adapt under pressure

Respond to the changed setup, collect final readings and defend findings.

AI ACCESS

Who can ask AI—and when?

Two adult-operated AI Desk visits, with up to two prompts each. Round one explores designs; round two examines actual test failures. No AI after the surprise.

PILOT CHALLENGE

What will VGS test first?

The first pilot candidate is Rainwater Rescue: build a gravity-fed collection device using limited materials and evaluate real water transfer. Teams compare repeated measurements and adapt to a changed condition.

HOW SUCCESS IS EVALUATED

Show the evidence.

The published rules will include criterion weights, standard test methods and judging arrangements. These are the present assessment priorities, not a final inter-school score sheet.

  • Measured final performance
  • Fair testing and iteration
  • Critical evaluation of AI suggestions
  • Adaptation to changed conditions
  • Independent student understanding

Before final approval: The water apparatus and difficulty still require a physical school-level pilot. Performance measurements must be repeatable before final competition approval.
