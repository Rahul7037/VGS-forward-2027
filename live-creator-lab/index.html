MISSION

Turn a brief into a digital product people can actually use.

Students turn a brief into an interactive digital product. AI helps them plan, test and improve, but their creation must actually respond to the user.

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN

A capability, not just a competition.

Each phase requires students to explain their own decisions. A plausible output without validation does not count as a proven result.

Plan

Turn a brief into a clear idea for a digital product.

Create

Build something interactive, not just a static design.

Test

Check that the product responds correctly to the user.

Improve

Use AI help and testing to refine the result.

THE STUDENT JOURNEY

From brief to evidence.

The step-by-step student journey, AI access rules and pilot challenge for Live Creator Lab are being finalised and will be published here after trial runs and school approval.
HOW SUCCESS IS EVALUATED

Show the evidence.

The published rules will include criterion weights, standard test methods and judging arrangements. These are the present assessment priorities, not a final inter-school score sheet.

  • A product that genuinely works
  • Response to the user
  • Quality of planning and testing
  • Independent student understanding
